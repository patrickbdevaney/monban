# Open-source steward attestation (draft, CRA Art 24)

Applies only if a legal person acts as open-source software steward for AEGIS (CRA Art 3(14)): one that
systematically supports the development of free and open-source software intended for commercial activities. AEGIS
is Apache-2.0 and distributed free of charge (operator decision 2026-11-11). No legal person acts as steward today,
so this attestation is not made. Who, if anyone, acts as steward later is `<operator to supply>`.

If a steward exists, it attests that it:

1. has a cybersecurity policy that fosters secure development and effective vulnerability handling
   (`SECURITY.md` at the repo root, `PSIRT-runbook.md`), including voluntary reporting of vulnerabilities (Art 24(1));
2. cooperates with market surveillance authorities on request (Art 24(2));
3. reports actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe incidents under Art 14(1) and (3) to the extent it is
   involved in development, from 2027-12-11 (Art 24(3));
4. makes the SBOM, the support period statement and the CSAF/VEX advisories available for each release.
