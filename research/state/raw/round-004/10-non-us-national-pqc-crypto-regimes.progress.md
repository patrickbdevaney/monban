## S1 BSI TR-02102-1 v2026-01 (2026-01-23) [read, pdftotext, lang=EN official translation; DE original exists]
URL https://www.bsi.bund.de/SharedDocs/Downloads/EN/BSI/Publications/TechGuidelines/TG02102/BSI-TR-02102-1.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=15
- KEMs recommended: FrodoKEM-976/1344 (Table 2.5), Classic McEliece mceliece460896/6688128/8192128 (+f) (Table 2.6), ML-KEM-768 AND ML-KEM-1024 (Table 2.7). HQC noted as NIST-selected, not yet recommended (sec 2.4.4).
- Hybrid: "currently only recommends hybrid use" of PQ KEM w/ classical (sec 2.2); hybridisation KDFs: CatKDF (SP800-56C?) [38] w/ KMAC or HKDF, KeyCombine [113].
- Deadlines: classical-only key agreement recommended until end-2031; migration to PQ KEM "by end of 2030"; classical signatures recommended until end 2035 (follows EU roadmap); DSA until 2029. QKD not recommended.
- Signatures: ML-DSA-65 and ML-DSA-87 (hedged; ML-DSA-44 NOT listed), SLH-DSA 192s/f,256s/f SHA2+SHAKE (128 not listed), LMS/HSS, XMSS/XMSSMT (sec 5.3.4.3). PQ signature recommended ONLY combined with classical signature (concatenation) except hash-based (can be used alone).

## S5 ANSSI-PG-083 v3.00 (2026-03-20) "Règles et recommandations ... mécanismes cryptographiques" [read, FR, pdftotext] 
URL https://messervices.cyber.gouv.fr/documents-guides/anssi-guide-mecanismes-crypto-3.00.pdf  (first edition to take quantum threat into account)

## S6 ASD ISM Sept 2026 (release v2026.09.4, 2026-09-03; "Guidelines for Cryptography") [read; github.com/AustralianCyberSecurityCentre/ism-oscal PDF; cyber.gov.au itself timed out from this host]
URL https://github.com/AustralianCyberSecurityCentre/ism-oscal/releases/download/v2026.09.4/v2026.09.03-pdf.pdf ; releases 2026.06.18, 2026.03.24 also exist
- AACA list: DH, ECDH, ECDSA, ML-DSA, ML-KEM, RSA (+SHA-2, AES; SHA-3 only inside ML-DSA/ML-KEM). NO SLH-DSA, NO LMS/XMSS in approved list (grep 0 hits) -> conflicts with BSI/ANSSI/CNSA which allow hash-based.
- ML-KEM-768 & -1024 approved; "ML-KEM-768 will not be approved beyond 2030"; ISM-1995 (Dec-24): use 768 or 1024, preferably 1024. ML-DSA-65/87 approved, "ML-DSA-65 will not be approved beyond 2030"; prefer 87; hedged preferred (ML-DSA-44 not approved).
- DH, ECDH, ECDSA, RSA "will not be approved beyond 2030" (ISM-0474/0475/0476 etc). NC/OS/P/S/TS all.
- ISM-1917 (Rev3, Sep-25): new crypto equipment/apps/libs must support ML-DSA-87, ML-KEM-1024, SHA-384, SHA-512, AES-256 by no later than 2030. ISM-2073: PQC transition plan (Sep-25).
- Hybrid: "not recommended; however, not prohibited"; ISM-1996: if hybrid used, PQ or traditional or both must be AACA. "no practical value in presence of CRQC". => OPPOSITE of ANSSI/BSI (hybrid required).
- ECDH curves: SP800-186 curves; Secret: P-256/384/521 pref P-384; TS: P-384/P-521 pref P-384.
- ISM TLS: ISM-1139 TLS1.3 only ("latest"), ISM-1369 "AES-GCM is used for encryption of TLS connections" (ChaCha20-Poly1305 not listed), ISM-1372 ephemeral DH/ECDH (rev4 Sep-26), ISM-1453 PFS.

## S10 CCCS ITSP.40.111 v5 (effective 2026-05-29; v4 2025-03-05) [read via curl html]
URL https://www.cyber.gc.ca/en/guidance/cryptographic-algorithms-unclassified-protected-and-protected-b-information-itsp40111
- Recommends ML-KEM-512/768/1024 (all), ML-DSA-44/65/87 (all), SLH-DSA all 12 sets, stateful LMS/XMSS only when signer can manage state (SP 800-208).
